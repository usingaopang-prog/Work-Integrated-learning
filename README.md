# Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) Portfolio

**Program:** Work-Integrated Learning at Richfield  
**Duration:** 4 Weeks (4 September – 30 October 2026)  
**Focus:** Industry experience in software evaluation, UI design, blockchain technology, hardware diagnostics, and website restoration

---

## Program Overview

This repository documents my four-week Work-Integrated Learning placement, completed as part of my qualification at Richfield. Over the four weeks, I was involved in real-world projects spanning website testing, UI design, blockchain certification, hardware diagnostics, and full-scale website restoration.

The experience provided hands-on exposure to industry tools, team collaboration, professional reporting standards, and the day-to-day realities of working within a software development environment.

---

## Week-by-Week Summary

### Week 1: Foundations & Discovery (4 – 16 September 2026)

The first week focused on evaluating existing systems and building foundational knowledge.

- **Zonke Website Evaluation:** Conducted a full functionality test of the Zonke freelance website and documented all issues found in a formal report.
- **Haskell & Coxy Wallet:** Introduced to Haskell, the programming language behind the Coxy Wallet, and began integrating it into my portfolio.
- **Blockchain Introduction:** Attended a Coxy Wallet event led by Mr. Bernard Sebanda, where I gained my first exposure to blockchain technology and the importance of continuous learning.
- **Hardware Inspection:** Under the guidance of facilitator Mr. F. Kato, inspected office hardware and co-authored a report on the findings.
- **Donation App Progress:** Participated in a progress meeting on the team's donation app, contributing to software development discussions.

---

### Week 2: Design & Certification (18 – 27 September 2026)

Week 2 shifted toward hands-on design work and formal certification.

- **Hardware Diagnostics Report:** Compiled and submitted the hardware diagnostics report from the previous week's inspection.
- **Zonke UI Design:** Collaborated with Venesa to design the user interface for the updated Zonke website, marking the beginning of a larger software engineering project.
- **UI Deployment:** Completed the UI design and uploaded it via my GitHub profile, finalizing a key phase of the software regeneration.
- **Blockchain Cardano Silver Wallet:** Took the initiative to complete the Blockchain Cardano Silver Wallet certification, gaining full proficiency with the Coxy Wallet.
- **Progress Review:** Met with Mr. Bernard to discuss the certification completion and overall WIL progress.

---

### Week 3: Restoration & New Platforms (28 September – 12 October 2026)

Week 3 involved deeper technical work and the introduction of new tools.

- **UI Handover:** Ensured the UI handed off to the back-end team aligned with the project planning and requirements.
- **Office Troubleshooting:** Mr. Sebanda installed a new Wi-Fi router to improve internet speed, followed by a meeting on the Zonke website, CBCA certification, and WIL student requirements.
- **Website Restoration & New Tools:** Began restoration work on the Zonke website and was introduced to cPanel and Laravel—platforms entirely new to my academic experience.
- **Code Restoration Planning:** Requested and led a meeting on the software restoration of Zonke, addressing how to adjust the PHP-based codebase.
- **Local Deployment:** Worked on getting Zonke to run locally as part of the restoration process.

---

### Week 4: Testing & Finalization (16 – 30 October 2026)

The final week focused on testing, quality assurance, and wrapping up outstanding tasks.

- **Zonke Development:** Continued active development and refinement of the Zonke website.
- **Testing Meeting:** Participated in a meeting dedicated to testing and ensuring all applications were functioning correctly.
- **Final Hardware Diagnostics:** Conducted another round of hardware diagnostics and submitted a corresponding report.
